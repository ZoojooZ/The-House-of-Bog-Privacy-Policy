Privacy Policy — The House of Bog

Effective date: [2026-10-29]

The House of Bog ("the app", "the game") is developed by Zoojooz ("we", "us", "the developer"). This policy explains what data the app interacts with and why.

Summary

  • The app contains no advertising SDKs, no analytics SDKs, and no third-party trackers.

  • We do not operate any servers of our own. We do not collect, see, or store any personal data ourselves.


What data the app collects

The app itself does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on any server we control. Your progress through the game (which level you've reached, which postcards you've sent or kept) is stored only in the app's local storage on your device.

Advertising and analytics

This app does not display advertisements and does not integrate any advertising SDK. It does not integrate any analytics or crash-reporting SDK of our own. No advertising identifiers are collected by us.

Children's privacy

The House of Bog is a general-audience app and is not directed specifically at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data retention and deletion

We do not retain any personal data, because we do not collect any ourselves. Your local game progress can be cleared at any time by deleting the app.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example if we add new features that involve data collection. Changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
